Demo FAQ

Clear answers before a walkthrough.

A demo can focus on AlmondPay, SmartPlus, HydrationHQ, or the exact type of portfolio platform you want to build.

Can I demo AlmondPay before it is public?

Yes. The demo can walk through the escrow checkout flow, buyer and seller states, dispute logic, and the API shape for teams planning a marketplace or social-commerce checkout.

Can SmartPlus and HydrationHQ be shown with real app screens?

Yes. Both demos can use real app screens, with the walkthrough focused on the mobile app, login flow, role access, dashboard, and product roadmap.

Do you build portfolio websites like the ones shown here?

Yes. The portfolio section includes live screenshots of commerce, directory, wellness, non-profit, and traffic-safety websites. New builds can be simpler, cleaner, and more conversion-focused than the older examples.

What should I prepare before a demo call?

Bring the product goal, users, rough budget range, expected launch window, and any current website or app link. That is enough to shape a focused technical path.

Can Almond Systems take over an existing product?

Yes. The first step is usually a code, hosting, security, and UX review, followed by a short stabilization plan before feature work begins.
