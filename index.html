Flagship Product · In Development

AlmondPay

The escrow-secured payments & checkout API for African social commerce.

Nigerians lose billions every year to "pay first" fraud on Instagram, WhatsApp and marketplace selling. AlmondPay holds the buyer's money in regulated escrow and releases it to the seller only on delivery — turning every chat into a trusted checkout.

The Problem
  • Buyers pay upfront and never receive goods
  • Sellers ship and get charged back or ghosted
  • Social sellers have no trusted checkout — just an account number in DMs
AlmondPay
  • One link or virtual account locks funds in escrow
  • Funds release on delivery confirmation or auto-timeout
  • Disputes resolved with a full, auditable money trail

Escrow Hold & Release

Funds sit in a regulated trust account until both sides are satisfied — hold, release, refund or split with one API call.

WhatsApp & Link Checkout

Generate a secure pay-link or virtual account a seller can drop in any chat — no website required.

Multi-Gateway Orchestration

Paystack, Flutterwave, Interswitch and bank transfers behind one endpoint, with automatic failover and split payouts.

Reconciliation & Audit Trail

PCI-DSS-grade logging, settlement reports and webhooks built for compliance from day one.
