Demo FAQ

Clear answers before a walkthrough.

A demo can focus on managed care, collections, reconciliation, automation, white-label engineering, or an existing Almond Systems product.

Does SmartPlus support JAMB, WAEC and NECO CBT preparation?

Yes. SmartPlus is built as a JAMB, WAEC and NECO CBT preparatory mobile APK with timed practice, weak-area review, progress tracking and offline-friendly revision.

Can you support managed care, collections and automation in Nigeria?

Yes. Almond Systems builds managed care, revenue collection, reconciliation, automation, backend APIs, cloud platforms and payment integrations for Lagos, Nigeria and African teams.

What should I prepare before a demo call?

Bring the product goal, users, rough budget range, expected launch window, and any current website or app link. That is enough to shape a focused technical path.

Can Almond Systems take over an existing product?

Yes. The first step is usually a code, hosting, security, and UX review, followed by a short stabilization plan before feature work begins.
