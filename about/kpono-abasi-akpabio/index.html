About Kpono-Abasi Akpabio

Kpono-Abasi Akpabio founded Almond Systems, a technology business based in Uyo, Nigeria. His engineering work focuses on robust backend APIs, production systems, fintech and payment workflows, ecommerce platforms, mobile applications and business automation.

Technical areas include PostgreSQL-backed data systems, Redis, distributed messaging patterns such as RabbitMQ and Kafka, cloud operations, and reliable software deployments.

At Almond Systems, this approach supports services ranging from website design and hosting to WordPress maintenance, application development, payment integrations and practical AI automation.
