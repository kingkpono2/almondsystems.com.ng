WordPress Website Maintenance in Nigeria: What to Check Monthly

Keep your WordPress website secure, backed up and fast. A practical Nigerian business owner's checklist for updates, plugins, WooCommerce and SEO.

Published 2026-11-05 · Almond Systems editorial team

WordPress makes publishing and online selling accessible, but launching a website is not the end of the work. Themes, plugins, content, backups and hosting all need attention. A sensible maintenance routine reduces avoidable downtime and helps customers complete enquiries and purchases.

Back up before making changes

Set an automatic backup schedule for both website files and the database. Store at least one backup outside the hosting account so a server problem does not destroy your recovery point. For active WooCommerce stores, daily or more frequent backups may be necessary; a small brochure site may require less frequent snapshots.

Most importantly, test a restore in a safe environment. A backup you cannot recover is not a dependable backup.

Update WordPress, themes and plugins safely

Review available security and compatibility updates regularly. Use a staging environment for significant upgrades and test forms, menus, checkout and account login before pushing changes live. Remove unused plugins and themes instead of leaving abandoned code installed.

Avoid installing several plugins that each perform the same function. Prefer maintained tools with clear update histories and compatible versions. Use least-privilege administrator accounts and multi-factor authentication where available.

Protect customer journeys

A website can appear normal on the homepage while its contact form fails silently. Submit a test enquiry and check that the message arrives. On a store, test product variations, shipping charges, coupons, payment notifications and order emails.

Check mobile usability and whether staff can see new enquiries promptly. If your business operates in Uyo or serves customers across Nigeria, test realistic customer experiences including local phone formats, address fields and mobile payment flows.

Improve speed and SEO together

Resize photos before upload, avoid unnecessary page-builder effects and configure caching that does not break checkout or logged-in sessions. Review broken links, redirects, duplicate titles and important pages missing from the sitemap.

Refresh service descriptions when offerings change. Good content gives real answers, not repeated city names or long strings of keywords. Monitor Search Console for crawling and indexing issues and use analytics to assess useful visits.

Build a monthly WordPress checklist

Confirm that backups completed; apply vetted updates; scan for security warnings; test key forms and checkout; review uptime; check site speed; inspect SEO errors; and record changes. For a busy online store, repeat critical transaction tests more often.

If the site handles payments or personal information, document who is responsible for access, updates, monitoring and recovery.
